## SQL

**[International Student Mental Health Exploratory Analysis](notebook.ipynb)**
- **Technical Skills**: Applied PostgreSQL data aggregation (`GROUP BY`, `AVG()`) to analyze student survey metrics across cohort tenures.
- **Key Findings**: Isolated peak depression in Year 3 (9.09 PHQ-9) and highest acculturative stress in Year 4 (87.71 ASISS).
- **Decision-Making Impact**: Enabled university administrators to target counseling and outreach resources toward high-risk student cohorts.

**[World Bank Sovereign Debt & Repayment Aggregation in PostgreSQL](international_debt.ipynb)**
- **Technical Skills**: Applied PostgreSQL data aggregation, deduplication, and filtering (`COUNT(DISTINCT)`, `SUM()`, `GROUP BY`, `WHERE`) to evaluate World Bank international debt indicators.
- **Key Findings**: Identified that the dataset spans 124 developing countries, with China holding the highest total debt (285.8 billion USD) and Timor-Leste recording the lowest long-term principal repayment (825,000 USD).
- **Decision-Making Impact**: Provided actionable benchmarks for international organizations and policymakers to monitor sovereign default risks, evaluate infrastructure financing, and tailor debt relief programs.

## Tableau (Business Intelligence)

**[Debt-Relief Lead Generation Analytics & CPL Optimization](Debt_Relief_Lead_Quality_Diagnostic_Dashboard.ipynb)**
- **Technical Skills**: Applied Tableau Public (Calculated Fields, `COUNTD` deduplication, choropleth mapping, dual-axis bar charts, trendlines) and Microsoft Excel to clean and analyze 3,012 consumer debt-relief leads across acquisition partners, debt tiers, ad creatives, and U.S. states.
- **Key Findings**: Uncovered that Google drove 54% of total lead volume at just a 3.0% conversion rate—dragging down the 4.4% portfolio baseline—while partners like AdKnowledge (6.4%) and Call Center (6.3%), the $10k–$70k debt sweet spot (peaking at 6.2%), September ad widgets (7%–14%+), and top states (CA, TX, FL) significantly outperformed.
- **Decision-Making Impact**: Enabled the agency to reallocate paid media spend away from low-converting Google search traffic, optimize audience targeting, and justify a +20% lead quality lift roadmap to unlock a higher $33 Cost-Per-Lead (CPL) client pricing tier (up from $30).
